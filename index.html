Questions

Before you book.

Do I have to change software?

Usually not. Most fixes use what you already pay for. If a tool is costing you more than it saves, we'll say so and show you the numbers.

Are you an accountant?

No. We set up the systems and keep them running. Your accountant does tax and advice, and gets tidier books from us.

How long does a Check-up take?

About two hours with you, then the report within a week.

Will you see my bank accounts?

Only what you choose to share, read-only where possible. We never move money without your approval.

Can you work remotely?

Yes, over a video call with screen sharing. In person if you're nearby.

What if I only want one thing fixed?

That's fine. Tell us on the call and we'll quote just that.
