Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Short version: only what you send us, only to reply and do the work, and never sold.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

Martin and Finch is a small studio that sets up back-office systems for owner-run businesses. You can reach us at martinandfinch90@gmail.com.

This website

  • No cookies, no tracking, no ads. We don't run analytics or advertising tools on this site.
  • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page opens. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The contact form

When you send the form, we get your name, your email, your business name if you give it, your note, and the best time to talk. The form is delivered by FormSubmit to our Gmail inbox, so both services handle it on the way.

We use it only to reply to you and arrange a call. We don't add you to a mailing list. If we don't end up working together, we delete it within a year, or sooner if you ask.

When we work with you

  • Only what you choose to share. We ask for read-only or limited access where the tool allows it, through our own user seat, not your password.
  • We never move your money. We don't sign checks, hold your bank logins, or run payroll. You approve and send every payment.
  • Your files stay yours. The work lives in a folder you own. When we finish, we remove our access and delete any copies we kept, apart from what we must keep for our own records.
  • Confidential. We don't share your business details with anyone outside the work without your permission, unless the law requires it.
  • Health and client records. If your business handles patient or client records with special rules, tell us first and we'll agree how they're handled before we look at anything.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll reply within one working day and act within 30 days.

Children

This site and our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
